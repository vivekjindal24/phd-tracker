# Things to know

Decisions, limits and behaviours that a university buying Anveshan, its administrators and
anyone writing the SRS or user manuals must know. Each point says what happens, why, and where
it was decided. Add to this file whenever a change leaves something a user could not guess;
remove a point only when the behaviour itself changes.

## The PhD file (PRD F17)

1. **Each person gets their own copy of the file.** Every annexure follows the same visibility
   rules as its screen, so two roles downloading the same scholar's file can get different
   contents. A part the caller may not read is listed as "Not shown to you"; a part whose
   module the university has switched off, as "Not in use at this university"; an empty part,
   as "Not on record". For example, the Research Office does not see the copies of the
   synopsis and proposal, because it cannot open the scholar's documents on screen either.
   *(2026-11-19)*
2. **The index uses Anveshan's 16 headings for now**, not the university's own numbered list
   (Medi-Caps keeps 30 rows with its own form numbers: II-B, IV, V, VI, VII-A …). The
   university's own index is the next step. *(2026-11-19)*
2a. **The university names its forms.** Settings → Forms of the PhD file lists all 18 forms
   Anveshan prints. For each, the root admin may set the university's title, its form number
   ("Form VII-A", printed at the top right of the page, beside the annexure) and its "copy
   to" list. Anything left empty keeps Anveshan's. For notices, a department admin's list for
   one seminar or viva still wins over the university's. The seminar marking setting's "name
   of the form" is a separate label shown while grading; it is not printed. *(2026-11-21)*
3. **Some entries print as forms, the rest as summaries.** These print as forms: the
   appointment of the supervisor, the formation of the advisory committee, the comprehensive
   examination, the registration seminar report, the pre-thesis synopsis report, the
   coursework clearance, the submission of the thesis, the approval for the viva and the viva
   report. Only the forms of Medi-Caps Form V–VIII were laid out from the university's own
   paper; the others follow the same layout until a university supplies its paper forms. The
   remaining entries are summaries that print stored values as they are (for example
   "approved", "minor revision"). *(2026-11-20)*
4. **The viva approval and the examination board are one page.** The university's file keeps
   them as two forms (Form X and Form XI); Anveshan prints the board as a table on the
   approval form. An external examiner is shown as "Name withheld until the viva" to anyone
   who may not yet see examiner names. *(2026-11-20)*
5. **A damaged image never stops a PDF.** A specimen signature, logo or photographed scan that
   cannot be read is left out (the name under the line still says who), and the rest of the
   file prints. *(2026-11-20)*
6. **A form never shows a signature nobody gave.** A signatory's specimen signature prints
    only where they signed an approval in the portal. Committees, seminars and examinations are
    not signed in the portal, so their forms always carry blank lines to sign by hand.
    *(2026-11-20)*
7. **Names on the forms come from the records.** The Chairperson and Secretary of the DRC
    come from the department's research committee, and the Dean is the head of the faculty
    above the department. Where these are not recorded, the line prints with the role only.
    The advisory committee that signs is the one in office on the date of the event.
    *(2026-11-20)*
8. **A progress seminar has no form of its own.** Its paper is the progress report, which has
    its own PDF. *(2026-11-20)*
9. **The panel's answers print only for those who may read the review.** On the registration
    and synopsis seminar reports, the panel members' answers to the form's questions and
    their comments appear only in the copy of someone who may read the seminar review on
    screen. A scholar sees them only after the supervisor has finalised the review.
    *(2026-11-20)*
9a. **The synopsis report says "approved" only when the panel recommended.** "Recommendation of
    the RAC" prints the approval sentence only once the review is finalised and every member
    recommended. If they all chose something else, it prints that ("Recommended with
    changes", "Not recommended"); if they differ, each member's choice by name. Before the
    review is finalised, or when no one recorded a recommendation, it prints a blank line to
    fill in by hand. *(owner, 2026-11-20)*
9b. **The Exam Office sees the synopsis report and its result.** The exam team takes the thesis
    on from the synopsis, so its copy of the PhD file holds the pre-thesis synopsis report, its
    signed papers and the RAC's recommendation — but not the panel's answers or comments. It
    still sees no other seminar, in the file or on screen. The Library sees no seminar.
    *(owner, 2026-11-20)*
9c. **A seminar's notice is signed by the department admin, the HoD and the Dean** unless the
    department admin changes it (9d). The
    circular names the scholar, enrolment number, admission date, supervisor, department, topic
    and (for the synopsis seminar) the registration seminar's date. Only those who run the
    department's seminars (its head and admin, and the Dean above) download it, from the
    seminar page; it is also in the PhD file before the seminar's report. Each signer is the
    holder of that role in the department (the first by name if several hold it); the "copy to"
    list is Anveshan's own until it becomes a university setting. The date printed is the day
    the seminar was scheduled. *(owner, 2026-11-21)*
9d. **The department admin decides who signs the notices and what they say.** On a seminar's
    Notice card the admin ticks the signers from five: Secretary DRC, Department Admin,
    Chairperson DRC, Head of Department, Dean. They can be saved for every seminar of the
    department, or for that seminar only, with its own announcement, closing line and "copy to"
    list. Signers always print in that order, and one person holding two of the roles signs
    once with both ("Head of Department & Chairperson, DRC"). A rewritten announcement is kept
    as written: if the seminar's date or venue changes afterwards, the admin must update it (or
    clear it to go back to the generated text). Only an admin over the department may change
    these; the HoD sees them. *(owner, 2026-11-21)*
9e. **The attendance sheet names who is expected and leaves room for the rest.** It repeats
    the notice's announcement and the scholar's details, then a table (S. No., name,
    designation, signature) whose first rows name the supervisor, the panel members and the
    scholar, followed by blank rows: 18 rows in all, one A4 page. Larger audiences sign on a
    second printed copy. It is dated the day of the seminar, and the same people who download
    the notice download it. *(2026-11-21)*
9f. **The viva has a notice and an attendance sheet too.** Both are printed from the viva
    arrangement's page, by anyone who can open it. The notice gives the date, time, venue (and
    online link), the scholar, the thesis title and the examination board, and is signed by the
    Controller of Examinations (whoever holds the Exam Office role), the HoD and the Dean. The
    department admin changes this on the arrangement's Notice card, for that viva or for every
    viva of the department, as for seminars (9d); the Controller of Examinations can also be
    chosen to sign seminar notices. The attendance sheet names the board and the scholar.
    Before the viva, a copy printed by someone who may not see examiner names says "Name
    withheld until the viva" in place of the external examiner. In the signed PhD file, an
    approved scan of either replaces its generated page (point 20). *(owner, 2026-11-21)*
10. **The unsigned PhD file is for printing and signing.** It holds the generated forms only,
    with signature and date lines, and leaves out the specimen signatures and the uploaded
    scans. The signed one holds both. *(2026-11-20)*
11. **"Corrections made for the examiners" has no record of its own.** It is held as an
   uploaded paper on the thesis submission ("Modifications made at the examiners' request").
   *(2026-11-19)*

## Signed or unsigned downloads (PRD F17b)

12. **A form that carries signatures asks "Download signed or unsigned?"** Signed prints the
   signatures on record. Unsigned leaves them out and gives a signature and date line for each
   signatory, so the printed copy can be signed by hand and its scan uploaded. *(owner,
   2026-11-19)*
13. **The progress report and the PhD file offer the choice.** The progress report's unsigned
   copy has lines for the scholar and each advisory committee member, or for the supervisor
   when no committee is recorded. *(2026-11-19, PhD file 2026-11-20)*

## Signed papers for the PhD file (PRD F17b)

14. **Who uploads and who approves.** The scholar's supervisor, the department admin or the
   Research Office uploads the scan of a paper signed by hand. The department admin's and the
   Research Office's uploads count at once. A supervisor's upload waits until the department
   admin or the Research Office approves it, and they are notified. The scholar cannot upload
   or approve. Every upload, approval and removal is in the audit log. *(owner, 2026-11-19)*
15. **Every paper of the university's file has a place.** Each seminar holds its notification,
   report and attendance sheet. Each committee decision holds the DRC minutes and the notice of
   the DRC chairperson. The thesis submission holds its signed form, the examination fee due
   creation and the modifications made at the examiners' request. The viva arrangement holds
   its approval, the constitution of the examination board, the notification and the email to
   the viva examiner. The viva holds its report and attendance sheet. Every other entry holds
   one signed copy. *(owner, 2026-11-19)*
16. **The examiners' papers are kept apart.** The panel of examiners and the emails to and
   acceptances from the national and international examiners sit on their own entry. Only the
   Research Office, the Exam Office and the department admin see, upload and approve them, and
   their uploads count at once. The scholar never sees them, and neither does the supervisor
   or co-supervisor, even one who also holds one of those roles. *(owner, option a,
   2026-11-19)*
17. **The department admin sees examiner names in those papers before the viva.** On the
    screens, the department does not see examiner names until the viva. This difference is
    deliberate for now; it can be narrowed to the Research Office and the Exam Office only.
    *(2026-11-19)*
18. **One file per paper.** When there are two national examiners, both letters go in as one
    PDF. Uploading again replaces the copy in use; the old one stays on record.
    *(2026-11-19)*
19. **Only PDF, PNG or JPEG.** The scans go into the PhD file as pages, so Word and other
    files are refused. A scan that cannot be read (locked, damaged, a blank page) leaves a note
    in the file instead of breaking it. *(2026-11-19)*
20. **An approved paper marks its entry "Signed" in the index**, and its pages follow the
    entry in the downloaded file, in the order listed in point 15. *(2026-11-19)*
    A seminar's notice, report and attendance sheet, and a viva's notice and attendance sheet,
    are generated pages until their signed
    scan is approved; from then the signed file carries the scan in that page's place, and
    the generated page is left out (the unsigned file always carries the generated pages).
    If the scan cannot be fetched at download time, the generated page stays. Other entries
    still print their generated form and then their scans. *(owner, 2026-11-21)*
21. **Anveshan does not send the emails to examiners.** It keeps the uploaded copies of the
    emails and acceptances the office sent and received outside the portal. *(2026-11-19)*

22. **The coursework mark sheet comes from the exam cell.** The Exam Office uploads it, and so
    may the department admin and the Research Office; theirs counts at once. A scholar may
    upload their own mark sheet, which waits until the department admin approves it. The
    supervisor does not upload it. The courses and grades typed into Anveshan are separate
    from the uploaded mark sheet; the mark sheet is the exam cell's record.
    *(owner, 2026-11-20)*
23. **Signature lines are a standard length.** On every generated form, each line is about the
    width of a signed name; a single forwarding officer signs at the right, as on the paper
    forms. *(owner, 2026-11-20)*

## Setting up a real installation

24. **Two-factor sign-in and signing approvals are off by default.** They are off for the
    demo. The owner wants both switched on at every real university installation (Settings).
    *(owner)*
25. **The university types in its own seminar marking questions.** The Form VII / VIII
    questions used to mark registration and synopsis seminars are entered in Settings; the
    demo ships with sample questions. *(owner)*
26. **Specimen signatures are confirmed by someone else.** A person's own upload waits until
    an admin over their unit or the Research Office confirms it; unit heads cannot confirm.
    *(2026-11-18)*
