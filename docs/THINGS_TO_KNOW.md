# Things to know

Decisions, limits and behaviours that a university buying Anveshan, its administrators and
anyone writing the SRS or user manuals must know. Each point says what happens, why, and where
it was decided. Add to this file whenever a change leaves something a user could not guess;
remove a point only when the behaviour itself changes.

## The PhD file (PRD F17)

1. **Each person gets their own copy of the file.** Every annexure follows the same visibility
   rules as its screen, so two roles downloading the same scholar's file can get different
   contents. A part the caller may not read is listed as "Not shown to you"; a part whose
   module the university has switched off, as "Not in use at this university"; an empty part,
   as "Not on record". For example, the Research Office does not see the copies of the
   synopsis and proposal, because it cannot open the scholar's documents on screen either.
   *(2026-11-19)*
2. **The university can print the file in its own index.** Out of the box the PhD file uses
   Anveshan's 16 annexures. In Settings → Index of the PhD file the root admin writes the
   university's own rows (Medi-Caps keeps 30), in its order and numbering, and fills each
   from Anveshan's records: a whole annexure, one generated form (with its signed scan), or
   one uploaded paper. Rules: each record fills one row only, and a whole annexure cannot sit
   in one row while a form of it sits in another, so no page prints twice. A row with no
   record prints "Not held in Anveshan" (Medi-Caps' Form IX, which Anveshan does not keep;
   a second "Form V" row for a re-formed committee, since every version of the committee
   prints in the one Form V row). Whatever no row takes is printed after the university's
   rows under Anveshan's headings, so nothing is left out. Only the printed file follows the
   index; the screens and the upload places keep Anveshan's annexures. *(2026-11-21)*
2a. **The university names its forms.** Settings → Forms of the PhD file lists all 18 forms
   Anveshan prints. For each, the root admin may set the university's title, its form number
   ("Form VII-A", printed at the top right of the page, beside the annexure) and its "copy
   to" list. Anything left empty keeps Anveshan's. For notices, a department admin's list for
   one seminar or viva still wins over the university's. The seminar marking setting's "name
   of the form" is a separate label shown while grading; it is not printed. *(2026-11-21)*
3. **Some entries print as forms, the rest as summaries.** These print as forms: the
   appointment of the supervisor, the formation of the advisory committee, the comprehensive
   examination, the registration seminar report, the pre-thesis synopsis report, the
   coursework clearance, the submission of the thesis, the approval for the viva and the viva
   report. Only the forms of Medi-Caps Form V–VIII were laid out from the university's own
   paper; the others follow the same layout until a university supplies its paper forms. The
   remaining entries are summaries that print stored values as they are (for example
   "approved", "minor revision"). *(2026-11-20)*
4. **The viva approval and the examination board are one page.** The university's file keeps
   them as two forms (Form X and Form XI); Anveshan prints the board as a table on the
   approval form. An external examiner is shown as "Name withheld until the viva" to anyone
   who may not yet see examiner names. *(2026-11-20)*
5. **A damaged image never stops a PDF.** A specimen signature, logo or photographed scan that
   cannot be read is left out (the name under the line still says who), and the rest of the
   file prints. *(2026-11-20)*
6. **A form never shows a signature nobody gave.** A signatory's specimen signature prints
    only where they signed an approval in the portal. Committees, seminars and examinations are
    not signed in the portal, so their forms always carry blank lines to sign by hand.
    *(2026-11-20)*
7. **Names on the forms come from the records.** The Chairperson and Secretary of the DRC
    come from the department's research committee, and the Dean is the head of the faculty
    above the department. Where these are not recorded, the line prints with the role only;
    so it does where several people hold the role and none has been chosen (9g), rather than
    guess who signs.
    The advisory committee that signs is the one in office on the date of the event.
    *(2026-11-20)*
8. **A progress seminar has no form of its own.** Its paper is the progress report, which has
    its own PDF. *(2026-11-20)*
9. **The panel's answers print only for those who may read the review.** On the registration
    and synopsis seminar reports, the panel members' answers to the form's questions and
    their comments appear only in the copy of someone who may read the seminar review on
    screen. A scholar sees them only after the supervisor has finalised the review.
    *(2026-11-20)*
9a. **The synopsis report says "approved" only when the panel recommended.** "Recommendation of
    the RAC" prints the approval sentence only once the review is finalised and every member
    recommended. If they all chose something else, it prints that ("Recommended with
    changes", "Not recommended"); if they differ, each member's choice by name. Before the
    review is finalised, or when no one recorded a recommendation, it prints a blank line to
    fill in by hand. *(owner, 2026-11-20)*
9b. **The Exam Office sees the synopsis report and its result.** The exam team takes the thesis
    on from the synopsis, so its copy of the PhD file holds the pre-thesis synopsis report, its
    signed papers and the RAC's recommendation — but not the panel's answers or comments. It
    still sees no other seminar, in the file or on screen. The Library sees no seminar.
    *(owner, 2026-11-20)*
9c. **A seminar's notice is signed by the department admin, the HoD and the Dean** unless the
    department admin changes it (9d). The
    circular names the scholar, enrolment number, admission date, supervisor, department, topic
    and (for the synopsis seminar) the registration seminar's date. Only those who run the
    department's seminars (its head and admin, and the Dean above) download it, from the
    seminar page; it is also in the PhD file before the seminar's report. Each signer is the
    holder of that role in the department (see 9g when several hold it); the "copy to"
    list is Anveshan's own until it becomes a university setting. The date printed is the day
    the seminar was scheduled. *(owner, 2026-11-21)*
9d. **The department admin decides who signs the notices and what they say.** On a seminar's
    Notice card the admin ticks the signers from five: Secretary DRC, Department Admin,
    Chairperson DRC, Head of Department, Dean. They can be saved for every seminar of the
    department, or for that seminar only, with its own announcement, closing line and "copy to"
    list. Signers always print in that order, and one person holding two of the roles signs
    once with both ("Head of Department & Chairperson, DRC"). A rewritten announcement is kept
    as written: if the seminar's date or venue changes afterwards, the admin must update it (or
    clear it to go back to the generated text). Only an admin over the department may change
    these; the HoD sees them. *(owner, 2026-11-21)*
9e. **The attendance sheet names who is expected and leaves room for the rest.** It repeats
    the notice's announcement and the scholar's details, then a table (S. No., name,
    designation, signature) whose first rows name the supervisor, the panel members and the
    scholar, followed by blank rows: 18 rows in all, one A4 page. Larger audiences sign on a
    second printed copy. It is dated the day of the seminar, and the same people who download
    the notice download it. *(2026-11-21)*
9f. **The viva has a notice and an attendance sheet too.** Both are printed from the viva
    arrangement's page, by anyone who can open it. The notice gives the date, time, venue (and
    online link), the scholar, the thesis title and the examination board, and is signed by the
    Controller of Examinations (whoever holds the Exam Office role), the HoD and the Dean. The
    department admin changes this on the arrangement's Notice card, for that viva or for every
    viva of the department, as for seminars (9d); the Controller of Examinations can also be
    chosen to sign seminar notices. The attendance sheet names the board and the scholar.
    Before the viva, a copy printed by someone who may not see examiner names says "Name
    withheld until the viva" in place of the external examiner. In the signed PhD file, an
    approved scan of either replaces its generated page (point 20). *(owner, 2026-11-21)*
9g. **A notice never guesses who signs.** Before every print, Anveshan checks who holds each
    signing role today: the Exam Office (Controller of Examinations), the department admin, the
    head and the Dean. One holder is named. Where several hold it (two department admins, two
    heads), the Notice card asks "Who signs as …" and the department admin chooses — for that
    seminar or viva, or for every notice of the department (seminars and vivas alike). The
    Controller of Examinations is chosen once for the whole university, never per department:
    by the Exam Office (on a viva's Notice card) or the root admin (Settings → Offices), from
    those in the Exam Office; department admins see the choice. Until someone is chosen the
    line prints with the role alone and the card warns. A choice holds only while that person still holds the role; someone who does not
    hold it cannot be chosen. The Dean line on the other generated forms (appointment of a
    supervisor, formation of the advisory committee) takes the Dean chosen for the department
    on its Notice card, by the same rule. *(owner, 2026-11-22)*
10. **The unsigned PhD file is for printing and signing.** It holds the generated forms only,
    with signature and date lines, and leaves out the specimen signatures and the uploaded
    scans. The signed one holds both. *(2026-11-20)*
11. **"Corrections made for the examiners" has no record of its own.** It is held as an
   uploaded paper on the thesis submission ("Modifications made at the examiners' request").
   *(2026-11-19)*

## Signed or unsigned downloads (PRD F17b)

12. **A form that carries signatures asks "Download signed or unsigned?"** Signed prints the
   signatures on record. Unsigned leaves them out and gives a signature and date line for each
   signatory, so the printed copy can be signed by hand and its scan uploaded. *(owner,
   2026-11-19)*
13. **The progress report and the PhD file offer the choice.** The progress report's unsigned
   copy has lines for the scholar and each advisory committee member, or for the supervisor
   when no committee is recorded. *(2026-11-19, PhD file 2026-11-20)*

## Signed papers for the PhD file (PRD F17b)

14. **Who uploads and who approves.** The scholar's supervisor, the department admin or the
   Research Office uploads the scan of a paper signed by hand. The department admin's and the
   Research Office's uploads count at once. A supervisor's upload waits until the department
   admin or the Research Office approves it, and they are notified. The scholar cannot upload
   or approve. Every upload, approval and removal is in the audit log. *(owner, 2026-11-19)*
15. **Every paper of the university's file has a place.** Each seminar holds its notification,
   report and attendance sheet. Each committee decision holds the DRC minutes and the notice of
   the DRC chairperson. The thesis submission holds its signed form, the examination fee due
   creation and the modifications made at the examiners' request. The viva arrangement holds
   its approval, the constitution of the examination board, the notification and the email to
   the viva examiner. The viva holds its report and attendance sheet. Every other entry holds
   one signed copy. *(owner, 2026-11-19)*
16. **The examiners' papers are kept apart.** The panel of examiners and the emails to and
   acceptances from the national and international examiners sit on their own entry. Only the
   Research Office, the Exam Office and the department admin see, upload and approve them, and
   their uploads count at once. The scholar never sees them, and neither does the supervisor
   or co-supervisor, even one who also holds one of those roles. *(owner, option a,
   2026-11-19)*
17. **The department admin sees examiner names in those papers before the viva.** On the
    screens, the department does not see examiner names until the viva. This difference is
    deliberate for now; it can be narrowed to the Research Office and the Exam Office only.
    *(2026-11-19)*
18. **One file per paper.** When there are two national examiners, both letters go in as one
    PDF. Uploading again replaces the copy in use; the old one stays on record.
    *(2026-11-19)*
19. **Only PDF, PNG or JPEG.** The scans go into the PhD file as pages, so Word and other
    files are refused. A scan that cannot be used leaves a page saying why, instead of breaking
    the file: it could not be downloaded from the file store, it is not a readable PDF or image,
    or it is protected by a password (re-save it without one and upload again). PDFs from phone
    scanning apps (CamScanner, Adobe Scan …) are locked against editing but open without a
    password; those print like any other. *(2026-11-19; locked scans 2026-11-22)*
20. **An approved paper marks its entry "Signed" in the index**, and its pages follow the
    entry in the downloaded file, in the order listed in point 15. *(2026-11-19)*
    A seminar's notice, report and attendance sheet, and a viva's notice and attendance sheet,
    are generated pages until their signed
    scan is approved; from then the signed file carries the scan in that page's place, and
    the generated page is left out (the unsigned file always carries the generated pages).
    If the scan cannot be fetched at download time, the generated page stays. Other entries
    still print their generated form and then their scans. *(owner, 2026-11-21)*
21. **Anveshan does not send the emails to examiners.** It keeps the uploaded copies of the
    emails and acceptances the office sent and received outside the portal. *(2026-11-19)*

22. **The coursework mark sheet comes from the exam cell.** The Exam Office uploads it, and so
    may the department admin and the Research Office; theirs counts at once. A scholar may
    upload their own mark sheet, which waits until the department admin approves it. The
    supervisor does not upload it. The courses and grades typed into Anveshan are separate
    from the uploaded mark sheet; the mark sheet is the exam cell's record.
    *(owner, 2026-11-20)*
23. **Signature lines are a standard length.** On every generated form, each line is about the
    width of a signed name; a single forwarding officer signs at the right, as on the paper
    forms. *(owner, 2026-11-20)*

## Change of supervisor

- **A supervisor changes only on approval.** The department admin appoints a scholar's first
  supervisor directly. After that, the admin proposes a change with the reason, the
  scholar's consent and the order; it goes Department → Research Office (the root admin can
  change this chain in Settings → Approval chains), and only the final approval makes the
  change, from the order's date or else that day. The old supervisor stays in the
  supervision history. A bulk import still loads supervisors as given. *(owner,
  2026-11-22)*
- **The HoD signs the Department step, never the proposer.** Another department admin cannot
  sign it either. If the HoD proposed the change, the Dean signs. *(owner, 2026-11-22)*
- **Work waiting on the old supervisor moves to the new one.** A progress report or other item
  at the supervisor step is then waiting on the new supervisor. *(owner, 2026-11-22)*
- **The co-supervisor is not covered.** A co-supervisor is still set or changed directly by the
  department admin. *(owner, 2026-11-22)*

## Grievances (PRD F19)

- **Who reads a grievance.** The scholar, the grievance cell and the Vice Chancellor, and
  whoever decides the appeal once it is appealed. Never the supervisor, the HoD, the Dean, a
  department admin, the Research Office or the root admin. A cell member who supervises the
  scholar does not see it either. The one way round this: the root admin appoints the
  grievance roles and could appoint themselves. Anveshan does not stop it, but the
  appointment shows in the audit log. *(owner, 2026-11-23)*
- **Two new roles to appoint.** In Settings → Offices, the root admin appoints the
  **Grievance cell** (the committee's members) and the **Ombudsperson** (UGC 2023 asks for
  someone from outside the university; they get a login that shows grievances only). Without
  a grievance cell nobody is told when a grievance is filed. *(owner, 2026-11-23)*
- **Who decides appeals.** The Ombudsperson by default. The root admin can choose the Vice
  Chancellor instead in Settings → Offices; the Ombudsperson then sees no grievances.
  *(owner, 2026-11-23)*
- **Deadlines.** The cell has 15 working days (Saturdays and Sundays skipped; the
  university's holidays are not known, so a holiday still counts). The scholar may appeal for
  15 days after the decision, and a decision nobody appeals closes after that. The appeal's
  decider has 30 days. A reminder goes 3 days before each deadline, and the day after a missed
  one the Vice Chancellor is told too. *(owner, 2026-11-23)*
- **Emails say nothing of the grievance.** A notification or email only says there is a
  grievance and links to it; the text is read in Anveshan after logging in.
  *(owner, 2026-11-23)*
- **Grievance emails cannot be switched off.** Anyone may mute other kinds of email, but not
  grievance notices: they carry deadlines. The notification settings page says so.
  *(owner, 2026-11-23)*
- **Home shows what waits.** The grievance cell and the Ombudsperson see how many grievances
  wait for their decision, and which are past the deadline. The Vice Chancellor sees any
  that are late. A scholar sees when their grievance has a decision to answer.
  *(owner, 2026-11-23)*
- **Demo data.** A newly seeded demo has a grievance cell member (grievance@demo.edu), an
  Ombudsperson (ombudsperson@demo.edu) and one open grievance. A demo seeded earlier has
  neither: appoint them in Settings → Offices. *(owner, 2026-11-23)*
- **Nor does the audit log.** The audit log never shows a grievance, not even that one
  exists, to the root admin, the Research Office or a department admin. Its record keeps
  who acted and when, but stores none of the words or the names of attached papers. The
  grievance's own page is its history. *(owner, 2026-11-23)*
- **Not covered:** sexual harassment goes to the Internal Complaints Committee (the form says
  so); grievances are not anonymous (UGC asks for a named student). *(owner, 2026-11-23)*

## Publications

- **Fill in from the DOI.** On Add publication the scholar pastes the DOI and the title,
  authors, venue, ISSN/ISBN, type and year fill themselves from Crossref. The scholar still
  checks each field and ticks where it is indexed: Crossref does not know Scopus, Web of
  Science or UGC-CARE, and the Library still verifies it. *(owner, 2026-11-22)*
- **The server needs the internet for it.** The lookup goes from the university's server to
  api.crossref.org. Only the DOI is sent, never a name. Without outbound internet the button
  says so and the scholar types the details. *(owner, 2026-11-22)*
- **Import from ORCID.** On Add publication the scholar gives their ORCID iD and ticks which
  of their public works to bring in; each is filled from Crossref and saved as a draft to
  check, mark indexed, attach and submit. Works without a DOI, and works the scholar's ORCID
  record keeps private, are added by hand. Up to 50 at a time; a long list takes a little
  while, because Crossref is asked one paper at a time. *(owner, 2026-11-23)*
- **Faculty record their own papers too.** Under Research → Faculty publications, any member
  of faculty (even one who supervises nobody and holds no other role) adds papers by hand,
  from a DOI or from ORCID. There is no approval chain: the Library verifies each one or
  returns it with a note, and once verified it is locked unless the Library returns it.
  *(owner, 2026-11-23)*
- **Only verified faculty papers count in NAAC.** The NAAC publication sheets add the
  faculty's verified papers to the scholars' approved ones. A paper a scholar and their
  supervisor both recorded under the same DOI is one row naming both; one recorded without
  a DOI cannot be matched and appears once per person who recorded it. *(owner, 2026-11-23)*
- **Attaching the paper is optional by default.** A university that wants the paper itself
  switches on Settings → UGC rules → "Require the paper file": then a scholar cannot submit a
  publication without it, and the Library cannot verify a faculty paper without it. Papers
  already submitted or verified are not affected. *(owner, 2026-11-23)*
- **Who sees faculty papers.** The author, the Library, the Research Office, the Vice
  Chancellor, and heads and admins over the author's department. Other members of faculty
  do not. *(owner, 2026-11-23)*
- **Not yet:** reading the DOI out of an uploaded PDF, keeping the ORCID iD on the profile,
  and checking the journal against predatory lists. *(owner, 2026-11-23)*

## Patents

- **A module of its own.** Switch on Patents in Settings → Modules. Members of faculty and
  scholars record their own: application number, filing date, office, and whether it is
  filed, published or granted. *(owner, 2026-11-23)*
- **The university chooses who checks them.** Settings → UGC rules → who verifies patents:
  the Research Office (the default) or the Library. That office verifies each one against
  the patent office's record or returns it with a note. Only verified patents count in the
  NAAC sheet. *(owner, 2026-11-23)*
- **A patent moves on.** When a verified patent is later published or granted, its inventor
  updates it, and it goes back to be checked. It is never removed once verified.
  *(owner, 2026-11-23)*
- **"Patent" is no longer a kind of publication.** Papers entered as patents before this stay
  as they were but count nowhere; enter them again under Patents. *(owner, 2026-11-23)*
- **Counted once.** A patent recorded by a scholar and by their supervisor under the same
  application number (spaces and case ignored) is one row in NAAC naming both.
  *(owner, 2026-11-23)*

## NIRF and AISHE (PRD M9)

- **Only the research-degree tables.** NIRF's Ph.D. student details, sponsored research and
  patents, and AISHE's Ph.D. enrolment, foreign students and examination results. The rest of
  both returns (staff, finance, infrastructure, UG/PG students) and AISHE's Ph.D. approved
  intake (seats) are filled outside Anveshan. *(owner, 2026-11-23)*
- **The Research Office and the Vice Chancellor download them, not the root admin.** They count
  category, disability, minority community and nationality, which the root admin does not read.
  *(security model, 2026-11-23)*
- **Two new profile fields.** A scholar's minority community and nationality (with the country
  for an NRI, a PIO or a foreign national). They are personal data, like category: the
  scholar fills each once and the department admin corrects it; the import has columns for
  both. *(owner, 2026-11-23)*
- **Each department's AISHE discipline is set once** by the root admin in Settings → University
  structure, as AISHE names it. Left blank, the column is empty.
- **How the counts are made.** NIRF "pursuing": admitted by 30 June of the year and neither
  awarded nor gone by then. AISHE enrolment: on the rolls at any time in the year (so a
  scholar awarded mid-year is counted); year of study from the admission date. AISHE
  "appeared": the viva was held in the year; "awarded": the degree was awarded in it. NIRF
  sponsored research: projects that received a grant entry in the financial year (April to
  March), agencies counted once whatever the spelling's case or spaces. Patents: verified ones,
  by calendar year.
- **These counting rules have not been matched against a filed return.** Neither form defines
  them, and no university's earlier NIRF or AISHE submission was available to check them
  against. The returns page lists them so the Research Office can compare them with its own
  practice before filing. At each new install, ask the office for last year's filed forms and
  run the same year to compare.
- **Disability means benchmark disability.** AISHE's PwBD is 40% or more under the RPwD Act
  2016, and the profile now says so.
- **Gaps are shown, not hidden.** A scholar whose category or gender is not recorded is
  counted in a column of their own; those with no admission date are named at the end, so
  their profiles can be completed before filing.

## Not in Anveshan

- **Consultancy.** Paid work faculty do for outside clients, and its revenue, is not recorded
  here; the university keeps its NAAC/NIRF consultancy figures elsewhere. *(owner, 2026-11-23)*
- **MoUs.** Agreements with other institutions or industry are a university-wide record (IQAC
  or the ERP), not a research-degree one, so Anveshan does not keep them. *(owner, 2026-11-23)*

## Thesis submission gates

- **The gates block; they do not warn.** A scholar cannot submit the thesis before 3 years
  from admission, nor with fewer approved publications than the university asks for. The
  Library cannot approve a plagiarism score above 10%; it sends the thesis back. All three are
  set in Settings → UGC rules. *(owner, 2026-11-22)*
- **Defaults follow UGC.** 3 years (UGC 2022), a 10% plagiarism limit (UGC 2018, Level 0) and no
  publications (UGC 2022 dropped them). A university whose ordinance asks for papers sets the
  number, and can count only Scopus, Web of Science or UGC-CARE papers. Only approved papers
  that are accepted or published count. *(owner, 2026-11-22)*
- **The admission date must be recorded.** With a minimum duration set, a scholar with no
  admission date cannot submit until the department records it. Check the admission dates
  after a bulk import. *(owner, 2026-11-22)*
- **The scholar sees the list first.** The thesis page shows "Before you submit · 1 of 2 met",
  saying what is still missing. *(owner, 2026-11-22)*

## Notice board (PRD F18)

- **Anveshan keeps no fees.** The university's own fee system keeps scholars' fees and
  payments. A fee notice goes on the notice board instead, as a notice with the PDF
  attached. *(owner, 2026-11-22)*
- **Who posts, who reads.** The Research Office and the root admin post to the whole
  university or any department; a department admin posts to their department (and the units
  inside it). A university notice reaches everyone; a department notice reaches that
  department's people and the heads and admins above it. Each new notice is sent in the portal
  and by email; anyone can turn off the emails ("A notice is posted on the notice board" under
  Email me when…). A notice with a "take it down after" date moves to past notices the day
  after. *(owner, 2026-11-22)*

## Screens

- **One date format for the whole university.** Settings → University identity → Date format
  sets how every date is written, in one go: on every screen, PDF, notice, form, email and
  reminder. Choices: 21 Nov 2026 (the default), 21 November 2026, 21/11/2026, 21-11-2026,
  21.11.2026, 2026-11-21 and 11/21/2026. Month names are always English and never follow the
  viewer's browser language. Times are written "4:30 pm" — on screens in the viewer's time
  zone, on PDFs and emails in India's. CSV downloads keep 2026-11-21 so spreadsheets sort them.
  A change shows at once for whoever made it; others see it on their next page load and the
  server within a minute. Only the root admin changes it. *(owner, 2026-11-22)*

## Setting up a real installation

24. **Two-factor sign-in and signing approvals are off by default.** They are off for the
    demo. The owner wants both switched on at every real university installation (Settings).
    *(owner)*
25. **The university types in its own seminar marking questions.** The Form VII / VIII
    questions used to mark registration and synopsis seminars are entered in Settings; the
    demo ships with sample questions. *(owner)*
26. **Specimen signatures are confirmed by someone else.** A person's own upload waits until
    an admin over their unit or the Research Office confirms it; unit heads cannot confirm.
    *(2026-11-18)*
